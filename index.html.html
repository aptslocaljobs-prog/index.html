Privacy Notice

What we collect: your mobile number (for login), and the details you choose to post: name, qualification, district, business name, job details and a contact number.

Who can see it: a local vacancy and its contact number are public, so anyone interested can call or WhatsApp the employer. If you report a vacancy, the reason and details you enter are shown to the site admin.

Your control: you can edit or delete your own posts at any time from your profile. The site owner may remove posts that are false, abusive or misleading. To have your data deleted, contact the site owner using the channels in the footer.

Please note: do not post anyone else's number without permission. Never pay money to an employer before verifying the job. We do not sell your data. Data you enter is stored only in your own browser on this device.
